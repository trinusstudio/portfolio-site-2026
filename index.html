How a batch moves

Five stages, one point of contact, no scope creep.

  1. 01

    Brief

    A short intake call or form covering product, angle, and what's already been tried. No landing pages or funnel work — video only.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Three concepts, each with two hook variations, sent for one consolidated round of feedback before anything goes into production.

  3. 03

    Production

    Approved concepts move into generation and edit. This is where the AI pipeline does the heavy lifting under a locked brand and character build.

  4. 04

    QA

    Every video is checked against the brief for product accuracy, brand fit, and story logic before it reaches you — the same discipline used on high-volume agency work.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Six finished ads delivered 7–10 business days after concept approval, with one revision round included.

About

I'm a founder-led AI creative studio, not an agency roster. Day-to-day, I produce high-volume UGC ad video for DTC and e-commerce brands, trained under a multi-layer QA chain — self-QA, team lead QA, then account QA — built to catch hallucinations, product inaccuracies, and brief mismatches before anything ships.

That same discipline runs through independent client work: locked character builds, brand-specific prompt systems, and a production process that treats an AI pipeline like a crew, not a shortcut.

Questions

What's included in a batch?

Three concepts, each with two hook variations — six finished ads total, ready to run as paid creative.

How long does it take?

7–10 business days from concept approval to delivery.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds — one consolidated round on concepts before production, one after delivery.

Do you build landing pages or funnels?

No — video production only. If you need funnel work, I can point you to who to talk to.

What happens after the first batch?

If it performs, a retainer is available as a next step — it's never the starting offer.
