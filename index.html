Questions

What counts as on-brand in the batch?

Whatever's already in your brand guide or past ads — tone, visual style, product claims. I lock those in during the brief so nothing drifts during production.

What's the revision policy?

Two rounds — one consolidated round on concepts before production, one after delivery.

Does AI-produced mean lower quality?

No. Every video passes four checks before it reaches you — self QA, team lead QA, APM QA, and client review.
